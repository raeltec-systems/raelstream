raelstream
An open-source broadcast studio for churches. One phone camera, one laptop, one stream to Facebook and YouTube, and it's calm enough for a volunteer to run.

01 · LOGO
The ring and the wordmark
The ring is an open signal: a light that's on, with room inside it. The wordmark is always lowercase Bricolage Grotesque Bold at −3.5% tracking. The ring takes the accent colour, and the wordmark always stays ink or paper.
raelstream
raelstream
Proportions
The ring's diameter matches the x-height plus the ascender. Its stroke is one-sixth of the diameter.
Clear space
Leave one ring-diameter of space on every side. The smallest lockup is 96px wide, and below that use the app icon.
Never
Don't fill the ring, colour it Live red, add a cross or dove, or place it on a photo.
02 · COLOUR
Neutrals, one accent, three signals
Sky is Raelstream's default accent. Each church's preset replaces it with their own colour, which is used on buttons, selection and the ring. Status colours never change. Each has a single meaning and always comes with a label, so a church accent that looks like a status colour can't be mistaken for one.
