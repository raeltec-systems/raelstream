# Sunday operator checklist

For the volunteer running the studio on the Dell. Keep this page open on another device, or print it.
If something goes wrong that this page doesn't cover, stop, breathe, and look at the **Health** panel:
it names the part that has the problem.

## 45 minutes before

1. Plug the Dell into power. In Windows power settings, stop it sleeping while plugged in.
   **Keep the lid open.**
2. Connect the Dell and the camera phone to the **production Wi-Fi** (not the guest network).
3. On the phone: charge it (or plug it in), turn on **Do Not Disturb**, and close other apps.
4. Plug the mixer into the audio interface (UMC), and the UMC into the Dell.

## 30 minutes before: Preparation

Open Chrome or Edge on the Dell and go to the studio address. Sign in (email, password, 6-digit code).
Pick this week's saved service, or **Start without a saved service**.

1. **Devices:** press **Show pairing code** and scan it with the phone. Check the words match on both
   screens, then **Let this phone in**. On the phone, **Start camera**. Wait for
   **Connected directly**.
   - Check **Camera framing**: *Fit* shows the whole picture; *Fill* crops to the frame.
   - "The phone is sending 15 fps": more light on the people helps most.
2. **Uplink test:** press **Run uplink test** (20 seconds). Use the size it suggests (720p or 1080p).
3. **Destinations:** tick where the service goes. **Facebook needs this week's key:** open Facebook Live
   Producer → *Streaming software* → copy the stream key, then **Paste** it here. If it says "looks like
   last week's key", check Live Producer again.
4. **Rundown:** check the names (lower thirds), scripture cards and announcement images.
5. **Audio:** choose the **UMC** as the input. Play something from the mixer: the meters must move and it
   should say **Sound detected**. Pick the routing that matches the mixer (Input 1, Input 2 or stereo).
   - No UMC today? Choose **Phone sound** to use the phone's microphone (or an iRig in the phone). Tap
     **Allow** on the phone if it asks.
   - Wear headphones and turn on **Listen (headphones)** to hear exactly what viewers hear.
   - **Lip-sync:** if the badge says *Recheck recommended*, record a test clip: someone claps in front of
     the camera near a mixer microphone. Step to the frame where the hands meet, press **The hands meet
     in this frame**, then **Use … ms** and **Save calibration**.

When the footer says **All ready**, press **Open the Studio**.

## 10 minutes before: private test

Press **Start private test**. It sends the programme to the Raelstream server only; **nobody can see
it**. Wait for "server receiving", check the Health panel, then **Stop private test**.

## Going live

1. Press **Go live…**. Check the places listed. Tick **Record a private copy** if the owner wants one.
2. Press **Start sending to 2 places**. The top bar turns red: **ON AIR**.
3. **YouTube** usually waits for you: open YouTube Live Control Room and click **Go Live** there.
   **Facebook**: in Live Producer, click **Go live**.
4. Open each platform on a phone or another tab. When you can see and hear the service there, press
   **I've checked the platform playback** in its row.

## During the service

- Keys **1–4** switch scenes: 1 camera + name, 2 camera only, 3 text card, 4 "We'll be right back".
  **Space** puts the next rundown item on air. Typing in a text box never switches scenes.
- Switching tabs is fine; the programme keeps running.
- **"Camera dropped · slate on"**: the phone lost its link. Viewers see the slate. The phone reconnects
  by itself; press **Camera ready: Cut back** when it appears.
- **"Only YouTube is receiving the service"**: Facebook stopped. Press **Retry** in its row. If it says
  the key was rejected, press **Fix key** and paste a fresh key from Live Producer.
- **"Mixer audio has been silent for 15 seconds"**: check the mixer. If the silence is intentional
  (prayer), press **This is intentional**.
- **"The USB audio interface disconnected"**: plug it back in and press **"… is back: Reconnect"**. Nothing
  switches to another microphone by itself.
- **Slate with a countdown**: the studio lost its connection to the server. Check the Wi-Fi. Press
  **Keep the slate up 5 more minutes** if you need more time.
- **"Upload congested"**: the church internet is busy. The studio lowers the quality by itself and puts
  it back later. Ask people to pause big downloads.

## Ending

1. Press **Stop streaming…**, then **Stop both**. Wait for "This service has ended".
2. End the broadcast on YouTube and Facebook too, if they did not end on their own.
3. Press **View report**. Anything marked *Failed* or *Not tested* is worth telling the owner about.
4. On the phone, **Stop camera**.
